Japanese News Dark Mode Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Japanese News Dark Mode is a Safari extension that applies a dark theme to the NHK News Web Easy website. This policy explains how the extension handles information.

Information We Collect

Japanese News Dark Mode does not collect, store, or sell personal information. It does not require an account, and it does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services.

Website Access

The extension runs on NHK News Web Easy pages so it can apply the selected color theme. Page content is processed locally in Safari and is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party by the extension.

Device Appearance

The extension uses your device's light or dark appearance setting to choose the page theme. This setting is read locally and is not collected or shared.

Data Storage and Sharing

The extension does not maintain a remote account or server-side profile. It does not sell, share, or disclose information about your browsing activity.

Children's Privacy

Japanese News Dark Mode does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to kyle@kylestanfield.com.
